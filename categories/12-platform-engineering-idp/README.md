# Platform Engineering & IDP Subagents

Platform Engineering & IDP subagents help teams create internal developer platforms that provide clear architecture, self-service workflows, and measurable developer adoption.

## When to Use Platform Engineering & IDP Subagents

Use these subagents when you need to:
- **Design an internal developer platform**
- **Create self-service golden paths**
- **Implement or extend Backstage**
- **Plan platform adoption and outcomes**

## Available Subagents

### [**backstage-specialist**](backstage-specialist.md) - Backstage platform specialist
Provides Backstage architecture, catalog, plugin, template, and adoption guidance for an internal developer platform.

**Use when:** Building or improving a Backstage-based developer portal.

### [**golden-path-designer**](golden-path-designer.md) - Developer golden-path designer
Designs opinionated, low-friction paths for service creation, deployment, and operations.

**Use when:** Standardizing the preferred developer workflow for a service.

### [**idp-architect**](idp-architect.md) - Internal developer platform architect
Designs IDP architecture, service boundaries, and self-service control planes.

**Use when:** Defining the architecture of an internal developer platform.

### [**platform-product-manager**](platform-product-manager.md) - Platform product manager
Defines platform roadmaps, adoption strategies, success metrics, and stakeholder alignment.

**Use when:** Managing platform priorities and developer adoption.

## Quick Selection Guide

| If you need to... | Use this subagent |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Build a Backstage portal | **backstage-specialist** |
| Design a self-service golden path | **golden-path-designer** |
| Architect an internal developer platform | **idp-architect** |
