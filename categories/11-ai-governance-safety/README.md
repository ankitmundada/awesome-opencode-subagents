# AI Governance & Safety Subagents

AI Governance & Safety subagents help teams design, review, and operate AI systems with clear accountability, appropriate controls, and responsible behavior.

## When to Use AI Governance & Safety Subagents

Use these subagents when you need to:
- **Review AI risks** before deployment
- **Design enforceable guardrails** for AI behavior
- **Assess responsible AI practices** such as fairness and human oversight
- **Audit governance controls** and ownership

## Available Subagents

### [**ai-governance-auditor**](ai-governance-auditor.md) - AI governance control auditor
Reviews AI governance controls, accountability, risk ownership, and deployment readiness.

**Use when:** Preparing an AI system for a governance or deployment review.

### [**model-risk-manager**](model-risk-manager.md) - AI model risk manager
Analyzes model risks, prioritizes failure modes, and plans mitigations for AI behavior.

**Use when:** Identifying and reducing AI model failure risks.

### [**policy-guardrail-designer**](policy-guardrail-designer.md) - AI guardrail designer
Designs enforceable prompt, tool, workflow, and approval guardrails for AI systems.

**Use when:** Defining controls that constrain AI system behavior.

### [**responsible-ai-reviewer**](responsible-ai-reviewer.md) - Responsible AI reviewer
Reviews fairness, transparency, misuse risk, and human-oversight design in AI features.

**Use when:** Evaluating an AI feature against responsible AI principles.

## Quick Selection Guide

| If you need to... | Use this subagent |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Audit governance controls | **ai-governance-auditor** |
| Prioritize model risks | **model-risk-manager** |
